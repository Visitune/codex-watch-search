COMPILATION OF CODEX TEXTS RELEVANT TO LABELLING OF FOODS DERIVED FROM MODERN BIOTECHNOLOGY1 CAC/GL 76-2011 1. PURPOSE The purpose of this document is only to recall and assemble in a single document some important elements of guidance from Codex texts, which are relevant to labelling of foods derived from modern biotechnology. 2. CONSIDERATIONS Different approaches regarding labelling of foods derived from modern biotechnology are used. Any approach implemented by Codex members should be consistent with already adopted Codex provisions. This document is not intended to suggest or imply that foods derived from modern biotechnology are necessarily different from other foods simply due to their method of production. 3.
